# Task 247: FVG V2 Parallel Channel Detection Contract

# Goal

Define and implement the deterministic geometry contract for the owner-specified FVG v2 parallel-channel pattern: two low anchors form one channel boundary, a same-slope translated line touches an intervening high, and all candles in the detection window fit inside the channel.

# Source Requirement

Owner requirement:

- Do not use ATR as the stop basis for this FVG v2 idea.
- In a window, connect one candle low to a later candle low to form a line.
- Translate a same-slope parallel line upward until all candles fit inside the channel.
- The upper line must touch a candle high that exists between the two low-anchor candles.
- The drawn channel lines must later be available for frontend display.

  - Test role: owns synthetic candle fixtures and no-lookahead geometry tests.

- Forbidden roles:

  - Do not change unrelated pattern detectors.

# Context

Current FVG v2 work uses FVG retest, trend score, Fibonacci, liquidity target, and stop-mode metadata. This owner request introduces a new geometry primitive: a regression-free, manually explainable parallel channel built from two low anchors and one intervening high touch. This task should establish the channel contract before entry/exit behavior is built on top.

# Scope

- Add a deterministic channel detector/helper for FVG v2 research.
- Work from completed OHLCV candles only.
- Search candidate low-anchor pairs within a configured lookback/window.
- For each candidate pair:
  - compute the lower line from low anchor 1 to low anchor 2,
  - require positive slope for an upward channel in the initial implementation,
  - translate a parallel upper line upward so all highs fit at or below the upper line,
  - require the upper translation to touch at least one candle high between anchor 1 and anchor 2,
  - require all lows to remain at or above the lower line within tolerance,
  - record channel width, slope, anchors, touch candle, and fit diagnostics.
- Return no candidate when any validation fails.
- Include serializable metadata needed by later tasks:
  - `schema_version`,
  - `window_start_index`,
  - `window_end_index`,
  - `lower_anchor_1_index`,
  - `lower_anchor_2_index`,
  - `upper_touch_index`,
  - lower/upper line slope and intercept,
  - channel width,
  - tolerance,
  - fit/rejection reason.

# Out of Scope

- Do not add trade entries or exits here.
- Do not replace the existing FVG detector behavior here.
- Do not persist results to the database here.
- Do not expose frontend overlays here.
- Do not implement descending channels unless explicitly included after the upward-channel contract is stable.

# Requirements

- The detector must be deterministic for identical candle input.
- Candidate validation must be no-lookahead: only candles inside the declared window are used.
- The upper boundary must be generated by translating the lower line with identical slope.
- The upper touch high must occur strictly between the two lower anchor candles unless the owner later changes this rule.
- Every candle in the detection window must fit inside the channel, within explicit tolerance.
- ATR must not be used to calculate the channel boundary, stop, or target in this task.
- Metadata must be JSON-serializable and suitable for later UI drawing.

# Acceptance Criteria

- Channel geometry helper returns a valid channel for a deterministic fixture matching the owner description.
- Invalid fixtures are rejected when:
  - upper touch is outside the two low anchors,
  - a high breaks above the translated upper line,
  - a low breaks below the lower line,
  - low-anchor slope is not upward.
- Output includes enough line geometry for frontend rendering.
- No ATR-based stop/target behavior is introduced.
